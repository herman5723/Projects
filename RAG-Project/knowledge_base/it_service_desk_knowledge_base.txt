NORTHSTAR SOLUTIONS – IT SERVICE DESK KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document ID: IT-KB-014
Last reviewed: 20 October 2026

TOPIC: VPN CONNECTION ISSUES
Employees who cannot connect to the company VPN should first confirm that their
internet connection is active. They should then restart the VPN client and sign in
using their company username and multi-factor authentication. If the issue continues,
they should capture the error message and contact the IT Help Desk.

TOPIC: PASSWORD RESET
Employees can reset their password through the self-service identity portal.
A password must contain at least 12 characters and should not reuse any of the
previous five passwords. If the employee cannot access the identity portal, the
IT Help Desk can verify the employee's identity and assist with account recovery.

TOPIC: LAPTOP SECURITY
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen locking.
Employees should lock their screens whenever they leave their desks. A lost or
stolen device must be reported immediately so that IT can revoke active sessions
and begin the device response process.

TOPIC: SOFTWARE INSTALLATION
Employees should request business software through the approved software catalogue.
Local administrator access is not provided by default. IT reviews requests for
licensing, security, and compatibility before approving installation.

TOPIC: SERVICE DESK HOURS
The standard IT Help Desk hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST.
Critical security incidents are monitored outside these hours through the on-call
security response process.
